Mechanism
Links carry their own plot colour, so the animation and the angle plot name the same thing the same way. Joints are hollow — a ring in the link colour over the window ground — so overlapping links stay readable when they cross. The dashed amber outline behind the cart is where the step counter thinks the cart is. In simulation without slip the two sit on top of each other; when slip is enabled they part, and the divergence becomes visible in the animation rather than only in a plot. A fading trail follows the link-2 tip — the only decoration on the canvas, and it earns its place by making the phase of a swing legible in a still frame.
